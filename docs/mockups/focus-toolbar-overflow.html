Why not a flat priority queue rejected

The first attempt ranked all fifteen controls and parked the worst-ranked one until the row fit. It fits. Watch what it keeps — computed live from the same ranking that version shipped with:

Home, App switcher, Shake and Rotate are four faces of one idea, and the queue keeps three and drops one on a tie-break between equal ranks. Same for Inspect. A user watching the bar narrow sees controls vanish in an order with no story behind it.
